YOUR MISSION

What Will You Do as a Campus Ambassador?

You will be the primary bridge between your university or college community and ETEchnovation 2.0 at RUET.

01

Campus Promotion

Share official posters, announcements, rulebooks, and promo reels on social media groups, notice boards, and campus student forums.

02

Delegation Formation

Encourage fellow tech enthusiasts, coders, project builders, and gamers from your institute to participate in competitions.

03

Participant Support

Act as the first point of contact for students from your campus regarding registration procedures, categories, and schedules.

04

Carnival Day Lead

Lead your delegation proudly at RUET campus on carnival day, connect with the organizing team, and celebrate the tech festival!
